# Politiko — Know Your Candidates

**Website:** https://politiko.org.ph/

Politiko is a Philippine civic-information platform designed to help voters learn about and compare politicians using publicly available information and documented records.

## Purpose

Politiko aims to make information about public officials easier to find and understand. It encourages voters to look beyond campaign promises and consider a politician's background, track record, achievements, and accountability record when evaluating candidates.

The platform is intended to inform voters—not tell them whom to vote for.

## What the Website Offers

- **Politician Profiles** — Information about politicians and their public-service records.
- **Candidate Comparisons** — A way to review politicians side by side.
- **Objective Scores** — A record-based scoring concept intended to help summarize relevant information.
- **Community Trust** — A separate view of community sentiment, distinct from documented records.
- **Record Submissions** — A way for visitors to submit information and supporting sources for review.
- **Vetter Applications** — An opportunity for people to help review and verify records.
- **Transparency and Accountability** — An emphasis on linking claims to sources and making information easier for the public to examine.

## Guiding Principles

Politiko is built around informed participation, transparency, and accountability. Information should be supported by credible sources, scoring should be understandable, and allegations should be clearly distinguished from established findings.

The goal is to help the public assess political records more thoughtfully and independently.

## Who It's For

Politiko is for Filipino voters, researchers, civic-minded citizens, and anyone who wants to better understand the records of people seeking or holding public office.
